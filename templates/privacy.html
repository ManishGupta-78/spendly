Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account, we collect basic information such as your name and email address. As you use the Service, we also store the expense records, categories, and other financial details you choose to enter, along with standard technical data like login timestamps and device/browser information used to keep your account secure.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to operate the Service — displaying your expenses, generating summaries and category breakdowns, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your personal or financial data to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time, after which your records will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable timeframe.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting and infrastructure. These providers only process data as necessary to deliver their service to us and are not permitted to use your data for their own purposes. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on your account page, and we'll be happy to help.
